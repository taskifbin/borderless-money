## Grounded differentiator statement (use this wording)

> "We checked the remittance apps our families use; none of the ones we found
> computes the 2.5% Bangladesh Bank incentive into the route choice itself —
> they show fees and rates, and the incentive is at best a footnote. If a judge
> names one that does, we want to see it — and we will say so on stage."

Rationale for the wording: the "no other app" claim is unfalsifiable from a
hackathon repo; "the only one we found" is honest, survives a judge naming a
counter-example, and converts the answer into a conversation instead of a
contradiction.
