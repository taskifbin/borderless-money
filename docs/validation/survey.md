## Questionnaire (draft)

1. How do you send money home today? (bank / exchange house / app / hawala —
   which one, and why that one?)
2. When you last sent AED 1,000, what was the TOTAL cost — fee plus the
   exchange-rate difference? (ask for the receipt if they have it)
3. Did you know Bangladesh Bank pays a 2.5% cash incentive on formal-channel
   remittance? Did you receive it last time? (yes / no / not sure)
4. Do you know exactly how many taka arrive before you send? (always /
   sometimes / never)
5. How do you decide which service to use? (lowest fee / best rate / speed /
   trust in the brand / it's what the family knows)
6. Would you switch to an app that shows the exact taka that arrives, including
   the 2.5% incentive, before you commit? (definitely / maybe / no — why?)
7. Would you use voice input in Bangla to start a transfer? (yes / no /
   sometimes)
8. What would stop you from trusting a new app with a transfer? (open)
9. How long is acceptable for the money to arrive? (minutes / same day /
   1–2 days / don't care)
