## Interview script (5 × ~20 min, in-person or call)

Use when the questionnaire is too formal — same question numbers map to the
results table above. Run 5 interviews before the video day if possible.

1. **Open (2 min):** "Tell me about the last time you sent money home." Listen
   for: channel, amount, who receives, how they chose it. Do not correct or
   pitch anything.
2. **Cost recall (3 min — Q2):** "What did it cost you in total — fee plus the
   rate difference? Do you have the receipt?" Record the exact figures they
   say; never estimate for them.
3. **Incentive probe (3 min — Q3):** "Did you know Bangladesh Bank pays a 2.5%
   cash incentive on formal remittance? Did you see it last time?" Record
   yes/no/not-sure and their exact words about it.
4. **Certainty probe (3 min — Q4, Q5):** "Before you hit send, do you know
   exactly how many taka will arrive? How do you pick between services?"
5. **Concept (5 min — Q6, Q7):** Show the xPay quote screen (phone). "This
   shows the exact taka that arrives, including the 2.5% incentive, before you
   commit. Would you switch? Would you use voice input in Bangla?" Capture
   verbatim reaction.
6. **Trust (2 min — Q8):** "What would stop you from trusting a new app with a
   transfer?" Verbatim only.
7. **Close (2 min — Q9):** "How fast does the money need to arrive for this to
   be useful?" Thank them; note date + channel.
