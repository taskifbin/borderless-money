# Stellar rail sensitivity — what anchor spread can the headline survive?

The `+3.05%` uplift headline (AED 1,000 → bKash, best route vs legacy SWIFT)
assumes the Stellar Hybrid Anchor quotes a customer FX rate about **1.0% inside
the mid-market rate** (32.8185 vs mid 33.15). Anchor spread is the one input we
do not control — it belongs to the anchor provider — so the headline is a
**scenario, not a constant**.

Generated by `scripts/stellar_sensitivity.py` (deterministic, exact-decimal;
rerun it after any corridor-config change — do not hand-edit these numbers):

| Anchor spread | Stellar customer FX | Best-route net (BDT) | Uplift vs legacy | Meets ≥ 3.0% target |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | :--- |
| 0.5% | 32.9843 | 33,610.95 | +3.63% | yes |
| **1.0% (headline assumption)** | **32.8185** | **33,442.05** | **+3.11%** | yes |
| 1.5% | 32.6527 | 33,273.15 | +2.59% | **NO** |

Basis (identical in every row): principal AED 1,000, fees 6.00 AED
(3.00 rail + 1.00 last-mile + 2.00 xPay platform fee) converted at the same
rate, 2.5% Bangladesh Bank incentive on eligible rails, legacy counterfactual
= Traditional SWIFT without the platform fee at ৳32,432.40.

## The honest statement (use this on slides)

> **The headline +3.05% holds while the anchor spread stays at or below ~1.1%.
> At a 1.5% anchor spread the uplift is +2.59% — below our 3% target.** The
> engine makes the spread visible per route (`spread_per_source_unit`), so the
> number a judge sees in the demo is the number this table is built from.

## Why the fee/FX structure still holds in the pessimistic row

The 2.5% incentive (820.00 → 816.32 BDT across the table) is computed on the
converted principal, and xPay's AED 2 platform fee is converted at the same
rate — so the platform fee *shrinks in BDT terms* as the anchor rate worsens,
but never disappears from the itemized breakdown.

## What would have to be true for a 1.5% spread

Nothing illegal — a 1.5% spread is within the observed range for anchor-based
corridors. If quotes come back at that level, the honest response is pricing
strategy (negotiate the anchor spread, or accept a +2.6% headline), not
changing the math.
