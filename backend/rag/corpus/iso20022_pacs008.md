# ISO 20022 — Cross-Border Payments and pacs.008 (excerpt)

Source: ISO 20022 payment messaging standard — pacs.008 FI-to-FI Customer Credit Transfer (excerpt)
Status: DEMO RETRIEVAL CORPUS — reference documentation.

pacs.008 carries the structured data for a financial-institution-to-financial-
institution customer credit transfer: message identification, settlement amount
and currency, debtor and creditor identities, and settlement method (e.g. INGD
— instructed). Regulatory reporting fields support travel-rule data where
required.

Using ISO 20022 payloads for remittance legs keeps the corridor interoperable
with correspondent banking and modern instant-payment systems, and provides an
audit-friendly, structured record that pairs naturally with an on-chain
settlement hash kept off-chain from personal data.
