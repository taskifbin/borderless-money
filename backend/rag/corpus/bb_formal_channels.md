# Bangladesh Bank — Anti-Hundi and Formal Channel Policy (excerpt)

Source: Bangladesh Bank remittance promotion policy (excerpts)
Status: DEMO RETRIEVAL CORPUS — verify against the original circular PDFs before submission.

Remittances sent through informal channels ("hundi") do not qualify for the
government cash incentive and carry no regulatory protection for the beneficiary.
The incentive structure and the 2.5% rate exist specifically to move flows into
formal channels, where transactions are screened, recorded, and traceable.

Digital remittance products that quote the full breakdown — FX rate, fees, and
the government incentive — directly support this policy goal by making the formal
channel the economically rational choice for the sender.
