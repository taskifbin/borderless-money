# OFAC — Sanctions Screening and Name Matching (excerpt)

Source: U.S. Department of the Treasury — OFAC FAQs (ofac.treasury.gov) — demo excerpt
Status: DEMO RETRIEVAL CORPUS — verify against ofac.treasury.gov before submission.

Sanctions compliance programs are expected to use a risk-based approach to screen
customers and counterparties against the SDN List and other sanctions lists.
Because names may be transliterated, misspelled, or reordered, exact-match
screening alone is not considered adequate; fuzzy-matching techniques with
reviewed thresholds are common practice.

Screening hits fall into three broad outcomes: a true match (block and report),
a potential match (hold pending review by a qualified analyst), and a false
positive (clear). Over-blocking — rejecting transactions without human review —
is discouraged; potential matches should be held and reviewed, not auto-rejected.

The 50 Percent Rule: entities owned 50% or more, individually or in the
aggregate, by blocked persons are considered blocked even if they are not
themselves listed.
