# Bangladesh Bank — Remittance Flow and Exchange Rate Application (excerpt)

Source: Bangladesh Bank, Foreign Exchange Policy Department (guidelines excerpts)
Status: DEMO RETRIEVAL CORPUS — verify against the original circular PDFs before submission.

Inbound remittances are converted to Bangladeshi Taka at the exchange rate
applicable at the time of credit. Banks and exchange houses publish their
effective rates; the interbank mid-market rate serves as the reference. Fees,
charges, and the government cash incentive (where applicable) are applied to the
taka amount, and the beneficiary receives the net credit.

Remitters benefit from comparing the effective (all-in) rate across channels:
the combination of the quoted FX rate, transfer fees, and the 2.5% government
incentive determines the final amount received — not the headline rate alone.
