# Bangladesh Bank — Remittance Incentive Guideline (excerpt)

Source: Bangladesh Bank — SME & Special Programmes Department (www.bb.org.bd), remittance incentive circulars — demo excerpt

Status: DEMO RETRIEVAL CORPUS — verify against the original circular PDFs before submission.

The Government of Bangladesh pays a cash incentive on remittances received through
formal banking channels. The incentive rate is 2.5% of the remitted amount for
remittances sent by non-resident Bangladeshis through legal channels, credited in
BDT to the beneficiary's account or mobile financial services (MFS) wallet.

Key conditions:
- The remittance must come through formal channels (bank, exchange house, or
  approved fintech/MFS partnerships). Hand-carried foreign currency (hundi) does
  not qualify.
- The incentive is paid IN ADDITION to the converted taka amount at the prevailing
  exchange rate; it is not a reduction of fees.
- MFS wallets (bKash, Nagad, Rocket) qualify as payout destinations where the
  remitter's bank or exchange house has a signed agreement with the MFS provider.
- The incentive is funded by the Government of Bangladesh and distributed through
  the banking system; the beneficiary does not apply for it — it is applied
  automatically at payout.
