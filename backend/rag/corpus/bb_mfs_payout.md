# Bangladesh Bank — MFS Payout of Foreign Remittance (excerpt)

Source: Bangladesh Bank, Payment Systems Department guidance on mobile financial services
Status: DEMO RETRIEVAL CORPUS — verify against the original circular PDFs before submission.

Mobile Financial Services providers (bKash, Nagad, Rocket) may disburse inbound
foreign remittances to beneficiary wallets. The taka equivalent is determined at
the exchange rate applicable on the day of credit. Disbursement must be completed
promptly after receipt of funds, and the wallet holder must be clearly identified
as the intended beneficiary of the inbound remittance.

For routing purposes, the payout leg to an MFS wallet is treated as a domestic
last-mile payment; the cross-border leg remains regulated under the foreign
exchange policy of Bangladesh Bank.
